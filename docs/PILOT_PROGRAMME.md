# ScaleLane design-partner pilot

**What it is:** three organisations run one full Program Increment (about 10–12 weeks) on
ScaleLane, from PI Planning to Inspect & Adapt, and tell us what works, what is missing and what
they would pay for.

**What partners get:** the product free, onboarding at a pilot discount (see
[SERVICES.md](SERVICES.md)), direct access to the maker, and fixes every two weeks.

## Partner slots

| Slot | Profile | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Azure DevOps Server 2022.1 on-premises**, ideally regulated or public sector (required) | Open |
| 2 | Azure DevOps Services, one ART, new to SAFe | Open |
| 3 | Azure DevOps Services, 2 or more ARTs or a Large Solution, or trains in several projects | Open |

## Entry criteria

- An RTE (or equivalent) sponsors the pilot, and a PMO / LPM stakeholder joins the final review.
- At least one ART with 4 or more teams, and a PI Planning event 3–8 weeks away.
- A project (or collection) administrator can install the extension; a test project is available for a dry run.
- The partner agrees to the feedback cadence and, if the pilot succeeds, to be a reference (named or anonymous).
- There is no SLA during the pilot.

## Timeline

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| −4 to −3 | Kick-off (60 min), install on a test project, configuration (Quick start) |
| −2 | Dry run of PI Planning on the test project; backlog check |
| −1 | Production setup; permissions and Business Owners checked; backup taken |
| 0 | **PI Planning** with on-call support; retro within 3 days |
| 1 to 9 | Weekly 30-minute call with the RTE; fixes every 2 weeks |
| 10 | **Inspect & Adapt** from the tool; final review with the sponsor, including price |

## Success criteria

| Area | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Adoption | PI Planning runs in ScaleLane; at least 80% of the ART's teams plan on the Team Planning Board |
| Artefacts | All PI objectives (with business value) and ROAM risks are in the tool; the confidence vote is recorded |
| Execution | The RTE uses Reports in at least 75% of ART syncs; dependencies are kept in the tool |
| Close | Predictability is reported at Inspect & Adapt from the tool; improvement items are recorded |
| Quality | No data loss; no blocker open more than 5 business days; Server 2022.1 works without workarounds |
| Value | The RTE rates "very disappointed without it" at 8/10 or higher; the sponsor names a price or a reason not to pay |
| **Retention** | **The partner plans its next PI in ScaleLane.** This is the signal for introducing paid tiers. |

## Test checklist during the pilot

The manual scenarios in [TEST_SCENARIOS.md](TEST_SCENARIOS.md) are run on each partner's platform,
with priority on: cross-project portfolios (XP checklist), the capacity page link and derived
capacity (CAP-09), and backup / restore on a fresh install (GOV-13).
